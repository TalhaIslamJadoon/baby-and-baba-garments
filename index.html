Our story

A little shop with a big heart 💕

Baby and Baba Garments is a family-run kids clothing shop in the basement of Sapna Plaza, Jhangi, Abbottabad. We stock baby and kids garments for ages 0 to 12 — from a newborn's very first outfit to school uniforms and party dresses.

Parents across Abbottabad know us for soft fabrics that are gentle on young skin, fair prices, and friendly service. Can't visit? Just message us on WhatsApp — we'll send photos of the latest stock and deliver to your home.
